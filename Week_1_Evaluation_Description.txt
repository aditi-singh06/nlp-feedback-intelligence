During Week 1 of the Data Analytics Specialist – Language Processing internship, I planned an NLP project titled “NLP Feedback Intelligence.” The purpose of this task was to identify a real-world language-related problem and prepare a structured, feasible proposal for an NLP-based solution. I selected the challenge of analyzing large volumes of unstructured customer feedback, such as product reviews, survey responses, and support comments. Manually reading every response can be slow and may make it difficult to identify repeated issues or changes in customer sentiment.

I defined the project aim and objectives, including text-data inspection, preprocessing, exploratory analysis, sentiment classification, identification of frequent terms or themes, and presentation of understandable results. I outlined a step-by-step methodology beginning with dataset selection and data-quality checks. The proposed preprocessing stage includes handling missing or duplicate records, normalizing text, and preparing the data for analysis while preserving useful sentiment information. For an initial model, I proposed a TF-IDF feature representation with a Logistic Regression or Naive Bayes classifier. A pretrained transformer model such as BERT may be considered for comparison if time and computing resources allow.

I also planned an evaluation process using a held-out test set and metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and a confusion matrix. The proposal includes a six-week timeline with milestones for data exploration, preprocessing, model development, evaluation, visualization, and final documentation. Potential risks—including privacy, class imbalance, bias, model errors, data leakage, and limited computing resources—are addressed with mitigation strategies. Finally, I identified expected outcomes and project limitations so that the system is treated as a decision-support prototype rather than a replacement for human judgement.

This task strengthened my understanding of NLP project planning, problem definition, methodology selection, evaluation design, and technical documentation. The proposal establishes a clear foundation for the practical implementation tasks in the following weeks.
